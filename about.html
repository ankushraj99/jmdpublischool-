About Our School

Building strong foundations for lifelong learning.

Our Vision & Mission

JMD Public School was founded with a vision to deliver world-class education with rich Indian cultural values. We focus on intellectual, emotional, and physical growth of every child.

Our curriculum combines academic rigor with hands-on learning, technological skill-building, and sports activities.

Academic Divisions

Primary Wing

Playgroup to Class 5: Activity-based learning, creativity, and foundational skills.

Middle Wing

Class 6 to 8: Analytical thinking, science & computer labs, and language building.

Secondary Wing

Class 9 & 10: Board exam preparation, career guidance, and practical knowledge.
